Paint correction — by stage

  • Spot polish (single panel) — a door, fender or bumper with light scratches
  • Stage 1 — light polish: removes light swirls and surface hazing
  • Stage 2 — full correction: removes moderate scratches and oxidation
  • Stage 3 — heavy correction: multi-pass compounding for heavier defects

Ceramic coating — what's included

  • Hand wash and decontamination (clay bar)
  • Light polish/prep so the coating bonds properly
  • Professional-grade ceramic coating, applied by hand
  • Cure time before the vehicle is exposed to rain
  • Entry tier — 3 year protection
  • Standard tier — 5 year protection
Not included
  • Deep scratches down to primer or metal
  • Dent repair or bodywork

Polishing can’t remove these — they need touch-up paint or bodywork instead. We’ll tell you honestly if a scratch is too deep to fix by polishing.
